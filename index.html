Presentation

Partial Colour Vision in the Working Environment

Why partial red–green colour vision deficiency does not limit performance in FMCG supply chain roles

Atha Rafi Nur Diansyah

October 2026

A plate everyone can read: blue and orange stay distinct for red–green CVD.

EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

The evidence in one page: colour vision deficiency (CVD) at work

Medically

Partial CVD is a stable, inherited trait, not a disease. All three cone types are present; visual acuity and cognition are unaffected.

Diagnostically

An Ishihara test “fail” shows that CVD exists, not how severe it is or what the person can do. Severity-graded tests often reclassify applicants as safe.

Globally

The US, UK and Singapore allow people with CVD to study and practise medicine, a far more colour-demanding job than supply chain.

Practically

Supply chain runs on ERP data, dashboards and text-coded labels. Where colour appears, simple redundancy removes the risk at near-zero cost.

The ask: assess fitness on the real tasks of the supply chain role, not on a screening plate alone.

2

Key terms: CVD = colour vision deficiency, often called colour blindness. Ishihara = the dotted-number plate test used in most medical check-ups. Severity-graded test = a test that measures how mild or strong the CVD is (e.g. CAD, anomaloscope).

WHY SCREENING MISLEADS

Proof: Ishihara detects CVD, but it does not measure ability

Illustrative pseudo-isochromatic plate (generated for this deck). Plates are built to catch even mild cases.

1

Very poor correlation

In 1,363 subjects, the number of Ishihara plates failed correlated very poorly with the actual severity of red–green loss. [2]

2

Able, yet failing

Many anomalous trichromats perform occupational colour tasks as accurately as normal trichromats, yet would fail conventional tests. [1]

3

One-third reclassified

When the UK Civil Aviation Authority and London Underground adopted severity-graded CAD testing, about one-third of applicants who would have been rejected were judged safe. [3]

4

Tasks, not plates

In Japan, people who failed mandatory Ishihara screening connected colour-coded cords without error. [3]

3

Key terms: Pseudo-isochromatic plate = a dotted plate that hides a number in colours that look alike with CVD. Anomalous trichromat = a person with partial CVD. CAD = Colour Assessment and Diagnosis, a computer test that grades severity.

Sources: [1] Barbur & Rodriguez-Carmona 2017; [2] Rodriguez-Carmona & Barbur 2017; [3] Tan et al. 2023

THE MEDICAL PICTURE

Partial CVD means one cone type is shifted, not missing

Inherited and X-linked

Present from birth; carried on the X chromosome, which is why it is far more common in men.

Anomalous trichromacy

All three cone types work. One is spectrally shifted, so certain red–green shades appear closer together.

4

Stable for life

Congenital CVD does not progress or damage the eye. Visual acuity is typically normal.

No link to ability

It has no relationship with intelligence, numeracy, planning, or analytical skill.

Key terms: Cone = a colour-sensing cell in the eye (S, M and L respond to short, medium and long wavelengths). Deuteranomalous = the M (“green”) cone is shifted. X-linked = carried on the X chromosome. Visual acuity = sharpness of vision.

Sources: [3] Tan et al. 2023; [1] Barbur & Rodriguez-Carmona 2017. Curves are a simplified illustration

GLOBAL EVIDENCE

Even doctors may practise with CVD in the US, UK and Singapore

United States

CVD is not a criterion for rejection to study or practise medicine.

United Kingdom

Not a criterion for medical school entry or for practising medicine.

Singapore

No colour vision exclusion for medical school; 7 of 10 ASEAN countries set no requirement.

India

2017: the Supreme Court called a blanket bar “regressive thinking”; the Medical Council of India agreed to lift it.

Japan

Medical universities excluding CVD fell from 55.8% (1986) to 2.3% (1992).

“Color-deficient individuals should not be denied medical school admission or a practicing medical license.” (Tan et al., 2023)

5

Indonesia is one of only two ASEAN countries, with Malaysia, that exclude applicants with any CVD from medical school.

Key terms: ASEAN = the 10 member states of the Association of Southeast Asian Nations. Medical Council of India = India's medical regulator until 2020. Practise medicine = work as a licensed doctor.

Sources: [3] Tan et al. 2023; [4] Supreme Court of India 2017; [5] Medical Dialogues 2017; [6] Colour Blind Awareness

GLOBAL EVIDENCE

Decades of research: people with CVD work successfully

1958 British birth cohort: 6,422 males followed from birth to age 33

Population evidence

Cumberland, Rahi & Peckham (2005) concluded there is “little to be gained” from continuing school colour vision screening.

Legal evidence (UK)

In Bessell v Chief Constable of Dorset Police, the tribunal found coping strategies meant red–green CVD did not substantially affect day-to-day activities.

Medical profession

CVD occurs among doctors at the same rate as in the general population, and there is no clear evidence that it increases medical errors.

6

Key terms: Birth cohort = everyone born in one week (here, March 1958 in Britain) followed through life. Employment Tribunal = UK labour court. Coping strategy = a simple workaround, such as reading a label instead of relying on colour.

Sources: [7] Cumberland et al. 2005; [8] Bessell v Dorset Police 2017; [9] Spalding 1999; [3] Tan et al. 2023

COUNTRY CASE STUDIES

Australia: a tribunal weighed the evidence and cleared CVD pilots

ADMINISTRATIVE APPEALS TRIBUNAL · PAPE (1987), DENISON (1989)

Colour-deficient pilots

The tribunal's decision was “a resounding rejection of the proposition that defective colour perception poses any threat to the safety of air navigation.”

Quoted from Pape's published account of the cases

AUSTROADS · ASSESSING FITNESS TO DRIVE

Private and commercial driver standards

Colour vision testing is generally not required for private or commercial licences, because “colour-deficient drivers usually adapt to the demands of driving.”

As cited in NRSPP Juleye Fact Sheet 3 (2026)

Versus Indonesia: until 2021, police rules barred anyone with partial or total CVD from a driving licence. Australia sets no colour standard for drivers.

Key terms: Administrative Appeals Tribunal = the Australian body that reviews government decisions. Austroads = the agency that writes Australia's medical standards for drivers. Signal-light test = a control-tower lamp test for pilots.

SUPPLY CHAIN REALITY

Where colour appears on a plant, a second cue already backs it up

Stack lights / andon

Fixed position (top, middle, bottom) and often an audible alarm; status also shown in text on the HMI.

Safety signs

ISO 3864 / ISO 7010 pair colour with shape: triangle = warning, circle = prohibition or mandatory action.

Pipe markings

Text legends naming the contents, plus flow arrows (e.g. ASME A13.1 practice).

Dashboards

Values, targets and variances are numbers; status can carry icons and text labels.

Stock & pallet status

Printed status text, batch codes and barcodes are scanned into the WMS.

Wiring & cables

Electrical maintenance scope, not supply chain; conductors are also labelled or numbered.

12

Key terms: Andon / stack light = a tower lamp that shows machine status. HMI = the machine's operator screen. ISO 3864 / ISO 7010 = international standards for safety colours and sign shapes. ASME A13.1 = a standard for labelling pipes.

Sources: [30] ISO 3864-1; [31] ISO 7010; [32] ASME A13.1; [6] Colour Blind Awareness

Accessible report design

Accessible palettes, icon sets and data labels in Power BI and Excel.

No cost

Text on every status

Printed status words or codes on tags, boards and checklists.

No cost

Colour identification app

Phone camera apps name a colour on demand; listed by the EEOC as a possible accommodation.

Free

Buddy check

A colleague confirms in the rare case where colour alone carries meaning.

No cost

Graded eye examination

One-time ophthalmology assessment to document type and severity.

One-time fee

Near-zero

cost to implement

Most of these changes also reduce errors for colleagues with normal colour vision, for example on low-quality screens, in poor lighting and on greyscale prints.

Key terms: Reasonable adjustment (UK) or accommodation (US) = a small change at work that removes a barrier. Power BI = Microsoft's dashboard tool. Ophthalmology = the medical specialty for eye health.

THE CANDIDATE

In my own words: working on a production floor with partial CVD

I have partial red–green colour vision deficiency. I work on a live production floor, and it has never stopped me from doing my job accurately, safely and objectively.

Operations Strategic Project, Philip Morris Indonesia (Link-up production floor)

How I work

Read status from systems

Uptime, yield, MTBF and waste come from data, where values and labels are explicit.

Use position, shape and text

Stack light order, sign shapes and printed legends, the same cues the plant is designed around.

Build accessible dashboards

My Power BI reports use labels and accessible palettes, which helps the whole team.

Confirm when it matters

In the rare case where colour alone carries meaning, I double-check.

Key terms: Uptime = share of planned time a line actually runs. Yield = good output as a share of input. MTBF = mean time between failures, a measure of machine reliability. HMI = the machine's operator screen.
